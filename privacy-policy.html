Tempix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Tempix is a cache and temporary-file cleaner for Windows and macOS. This policy explains what Tempix does, and does not do, with your data.

Summary

Tempix runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account, no telemetry, and no analytics.

What Tempix accesses

  • Files on your device. To find and remove caches and temporary files, Tempix reads directory listings and file metadata (name, size, modification time) in the locations you choose to scan, and deletes only the files you select. This processing happens locally and the results never leave your device.

Network

  • Tempix serves its own interface from a local server bound to 127.0.0.1 — your own computer. This traffic never leaves the device, and requests from other origins are rejected.
  • The only outbound links are optional “support the developer” links, which open in your browser only when you click them.

Data storage

  • Tempix stores its settings, and — if you use “Keep For Restore” — copies of files you deleted so you can restore them, in a local application-data folder on your device. This data is never uploaded.
  • You can clear restore snapshots from within the app at any time.

Children

Tempix is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
